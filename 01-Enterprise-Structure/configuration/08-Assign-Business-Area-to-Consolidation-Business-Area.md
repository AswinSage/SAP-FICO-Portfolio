# Assign Business Area to Consolidation Business Area

## 1. Objective
Document the assignment of a Business Area to a Consolidation Business Area.

## 2. Business Purpose
The source describes this assignment as grouping individual Business Areas under a common consolidation structure for consolidated financial reporting.

## 3. Configuration Activity
**SAP navigation:** `SPRO → SAP Reference IMG → Enterprise Structure → Assignment → Financial Accounting → Assign Business Area to Consolidation Business Area`

## 4. Procedure
1. Select the required Business Area.
2. Enter the Consolidation Business Area.
3. Verify the assignment.
4. Save.

## 5. Assignment Record
| Field | Project value |
|---|---|
| Business Area | Record from SAP |
| Consolidation Business Area | Record from SAP |
| Assignment result | Pending verification |

## 6. Validation
- [ ] Business Area exists.
- [ ] Consolidation Business Area exists.
- [ ] Assignment is saved and can be displayed.
