# Project Scope

## 1. Objective
Define the functional scope represented by the supplied SAP FICO implementation and business-process documents.

## 2. In-Scope Areas

| Phase | Functional area | Scope documented |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enterprise Structure | Company, Company Code, organizational units and assignments |
| 2 | Global Settings | Field Status Variant, Fiscal Year Variant, Posting Period Variant, employee tolerance |
| 3 | General Ledger | Chart of Accounts, account groups, retained earnings, G/L master and G/L processes |
| 4 | Accounts Payable | Vendor account groups, number ranges, tolerance, payment terms, reconciliation account, vendor master and vendor processes |
| 5 | Accounts Receivable | Customer account groups, number ranges, tolerance, payment terms, reconciliation account, dunning and customer processes |
| 6 | Asset Accounting | Chart of Depreciation, depreciation areas, asset classes, depreciation keys, account determination and asset master processes |
| 7 | Controlling | Controlling Area, Profit Centre Accounting, Cost Centre Accounting, Cost Elements and Internal Orders |
| 8 | Cutover and Testing | Master data, postings, reversals, payments and asset acquisition validation activities |

## 3. Business Process Scope
The companion process document contains 27 numbered processes:
- 8 General Ledger processes
- 5 Accounts Payable processes
- 6 Accounts Receivable processes
- 3 Asset Accounting processes
- 5 Controlling processes

## 4. Other SAP Modules
Sales and Distribution (SD), Materials Management (MM), Transportation Management (TM), and Production Planning (PP) are outside the documented FI/CO project scope.
