# Business Requirements

## 1. Purpose
This document organizes the business needs represented by the processes in the supplied SAP FICO project reports. It is a portfolio-level interpretation of the documented scope, not a separately approved client requirements specification.

## 2. Financial Accounting Requirements
| ID | Business requirement | Related portfolio area |
|---|---|---|
| BR-FI-01 | Maintain an organizational accounting structure for financial records and reporting | Enterprise Structure |
| BR-FI-02 | Apply consistent posting field controls and accounting periods | Global Settings |
| BR-FI-03 | Maintain a structured Chart of Accounts and G/L master data | General Ledger |
| BR-FI-04 | Record direct financial postings and review account line items | General Ledger |
| BR-FI-05 | Restrict and restore G/L account usage through account-level blocks | General Ledger |
| BR-FI-06 | Reverse incorrect accounting documents while retaining traceability | General Ledger |

## 3. Accounts Payable Requirements
| ID | Business requirement | Related portfolio area |
|---|---|---|
| BR-AP-01 | Maintain vendor master data and controlled vendor numbering | Accounts Payable |
| BR-AP-02 | Link vendor sub-ledger postings to the General Ledger | Accounts Payable |
| BR-AP-03 | Record vendor invoices and related liabilities/expenses | Accounts Payable |
| BR-AP-04 | Process vendor payments and review open/cleared items | Accounts Payable |

## 4. Accounts Receivable Requirements
| ID | Business requirement | Related portfolio area |
|---|---|---|
| BR-AR-01 | Maintain customer master data and controlled customer numbering | Accounts Receivable |
| BR-AR-02 | Apply customer account blocks and unblock when appropriate | Accounts Receivable |
| BR-AR-03 | Record customer invoices and related receivables/revenue | Accounts Receivable |
| BR-AR-04 | Record incoming customer payments and monitor open items | Accounts Receivable |
| BR-AR-05 | Define dunning parameters for overdue item follow-up | Accounts Receivable |

## 7. Acceptance Evidence
Each requirement should be linked to configuration evidence, master data, transaction documents, report output, or a test case. Do not mark a requirement complete solely because a documentation page exists.
