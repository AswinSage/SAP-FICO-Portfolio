# Accounts Receivable (AR)

## 1. Overview
Accounts Receivable manages customer master data, receivables, incoming payments, customer restrictions, and collection reporting. The project documentation covers customer account groups, customer number ranges, customer tolerance groups, payment terms, reconciliation accounts, and dunning procedures.

## 2. Configuration Scope
- Customer Account Group
- Customer Number Range
- Assignment of Customer Account Group to Number Range
- Customer Tolerance Group
- Payment Terms
- Customer Reconciliation Account
- Dunning Procedure

## 3. Business Process Scope
The companion Business Processes document covers customer master creation, customer blocking/unblocking, customer invoice, customer payment, and customer line-item reporting.

## 4. Documentation Structure
- `configuration/`: AR configuration procedures
- `master-data/`: Customer master and reconciliation-account records
- `business-processes/`: Source Processes 14–19
- `testing/`: AR test cases and evidence
- `screenshots/`: Redacted SAP evidence

## 5. Validation
Validate customer master data, invoice posting, incoming payment and clearing, reconciliation account updates, dunning setup, and customer line-item reporting separately.
