# Accounts Payable (AP)

## 1. Overview
Accounts Payable manages supplier-related master data, liabilities, invoice processing, payments, and vendor reporting. The project documents vendor account group configuration, vendor number ranges, vendor tolerance groups, payment terms, reconciliation accounts, and vendor master data.

## 2. Configuration Scope
- Vendor Account Group
- Vendor Number Range
- Assignment of Vendor Account Group to Number Range
- Vendor Tolerance Group
- Payment Terms
- Vendor Reconciliation Account
- Vendor Master Data

## 3. Business Process Scope
The companion Business Processes document covers vendor master creation, reconciliation G/L account creation, vendor rent invoice, vendor payment, and vendor line-item reporting.

## 4. Documentation Structure
- `configuration/`: AP configuration procedures
- `master-data/`: Vendor master and reconciliation-account records
- `business-processes/`: Source Processes 9–13
- `testing/`: AP test cases and evidence
- `screenshots/`: Redacted SAP evidence

## 5. Validation
Validate vendor master data, invoice posting, payment/clearing status, reconciliation account updates, and vendor line-item reporting separately.
